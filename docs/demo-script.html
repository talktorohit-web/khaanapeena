Demo tips

  • Voice ordering works in the Android app (allow the mic when it asks) and in the web app on Chrome/Edge. It's not on the PC app — use the phone or web there.
  • Stay on the happy path — don't open Settings or edit data mid-pitch.
  • If Wi-Fi drops, keep going — that's the point: “See? It still works offline.”
  • Let them hold the phone and place the QR order themselves — that moment sells itself.
  • Serving alcohol? Mention it in beat 5: liquor is outside GST and carries state VAT, and the bill shows both taxes separately — most POS systems make owners bodge this by hand.
  • Beat 8 is optional — worth 35 seconds only if they have a banquet room, take party bookings, or have regulars worth a membership. Skip it for a small takeaway counter.
  • Short on time? Cut beats 3, 6 and 8. Never cut 5 (payments) or 8 (day-end) — those close the sale.
  • The “someone else is paying” moment lands hardest with anyone who does office or party catering — mention it by name if they do.
  • A PDF opens the print dialog in a new tab; pick Save as PDF. Allow pop-ups once if nothing appears.
  • Owner worried about staff typing PINs all day? They don't have to. Picking the cashier when the register opens is enough to name every bill.
  • End on price, not features — “one bundle, ~half the cost, free trial.”
